Privacy Policy

This page explains how QISAH Creative Digital may handle information submitted through its website, email support channels and product-related communications.

Overview Information We Collect How We Use Information Sharing User Rights Contact

1. Overview

QISAH Creative Digital is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy is intended as a general statement for visitors, users and support contacts who interact with the QISAH Creative Digital website or related services.

2. Information We May Collect

  • Contact information such as name, email address and details you voluntarily send to us.
  • Messages, support requests and communication history submitted through email or support inquiries.
  • Basic technical information such as browser type, device information or page activity when technically available.

3. How We Use Information

  • To respond to inquiries, provide support and communicate with users.
  • To maintain, improve and present our website, products and business information.
  • To monitor service quality, security and basic operational performance.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to operate services, comply with legal obligations, protect rights, or work with technical service providers supporting the website or related digital infrastructure.

5. Data Retention and User Rights

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for business communication, support handling, legal compliance or security purposes. Users may contact us to request updates or removal of information, subject to applicable obligations and limitations.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at qisahcreative@gmail.com.
